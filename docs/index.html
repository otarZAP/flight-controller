Before you wire anything

The Teensy 4.x is not 5 V tolerant and has no protection diode on its I/O. Meter every signal line before it touches a pin — a receiver or ESC telemetry line idling at 5 V will kill the board on contact.

Props off for every first run of anything that touches motor output. No exceptions, including the runs you are sure about.
